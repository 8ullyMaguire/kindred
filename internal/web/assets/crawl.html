Add to the mirror

A walk finds works the mirror does not have by following AO3's listings, then queues them here for fetching. It runs as a command rather than from this page because a walk of a popular tag takes hours against AO3's crawl delay, and a page that started one could not show you how it went. 

kindred discover --seed "tag:Slow Burn" --enqueue

Seeds are also search:, user:, bookmarks:, collection: and series:, and --seed can be repeated. Add --print-urls to see what it found without queueing anything.
