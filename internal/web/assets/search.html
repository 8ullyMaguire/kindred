What this is

kindred is a general entity recommender over a read-only local mirror. It is a lightweight replacement for Concord, which used 800 MB to 1.2 GB; kindred does the same job inside {{if .Mode}}{{.Mode}} mode{{else}}a 220 MB budget{{end}}.

Every number on this site comes out of a local SQLite mirror. No request leaves the host, and nothing here writes to the corpus.
