Pool recall is the share of held-out works that were in the candidate set at all, at the depth shown. Recall above can never exceed it: when pool recall is small the ceiling is candidate generation, not the weights, and re-tuning cannot help. Overlap is the mean pairwise tag similarity inside each reader's list: 0.0 means no two works shared a tag and 1.0 means the same work repeated. Fandoms is how many distinct fandom groups a list held. These are the price diversification charges against recall: kindred eval --lambda 1 turns the pass off, and the two runs then differ in exactly these two columns and in recall. A row that says not measured predates the metric or was produced by a recommender that cannot supply tags — which is not the same as zero overlap, and is why it does not print 0.000. Signals blind names the signals that skipped for every candidate in that run. A run with idf_overlap there measured a different recommender from the one answering requests, so its recall is not comparable with a run where every signal contributed — three runs came back byte-identical for exactly that reason. Recall is the mean per-user recall, not hits divided by held-out works: averaging per reader keeps a prolific reader from dominating the number. A failed recommendation is counted separately from a miss — an outage and a bad ranking are different facts.

From /algo: a reader saw two lists and chose one. A draw is stored rather than dropped, or every win rate inflates as readers grow indifferent — and a draw is not counted toward the test, since a reader who could not choose has said nothing about which list is better.
